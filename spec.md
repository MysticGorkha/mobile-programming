PicVault
A lot of video editors and motion designers need quick import of images whether it is from browsers like google chrome, or from any file directory, directly to their editing software. Adobe photoshop allows this feature where we can copy any image and recent screenshot and directly paste into the software. But video editing softwares like Adobe After Effects, and Davinci Resolve does not support direct copy/paste of images. We have to manually download and import the files. And sometimes, finding the specific screenshot is very frustrating process. PicVault solves this by storing the recent copied images and allowing users to directly paste the image in any software they want (Davinci Resolve, After Effects, Premiere pro,etc).
